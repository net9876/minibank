## Operational payment review

1. Compare the payment record with the approved operational evidence for the same payment ID.
2. Keep the hold while evidence is incomplete. Escalate unresolved discrepancies to payments operations after the operator confirms a review case.
3. Record the evidence reviewed and the next owner in the case.
