## Beneficiary verification

1. Open the payment evidence in the approved case system and check whether beneficiary verification has completed.
2. If evidence is missing or inconsistent, keep the payment on hold and route a review case to beneficiary operations after the operator confirms.
3. Record the verification outcome in the case. Only the designated payments team can release the hold.
